## Pinned-issue

**arcus-provider-runtime v0.8.1 released — X posts work again**

What you'll notice as a consumer:

1. X post captures succeed again: full text for short posts, with links expanded, photos listed, and author/date filled in.
2. Long posts return the preview X publishes, with `extractor_detail["truncated"] = true` and `truncated_reason: "no_session"`.
3. Deleted or unavailable posts fail with a clear "x post unavailable" error instead of an empty result.

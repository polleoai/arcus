## Short post

arcus-provider-runtime v0.8.0 ships today.

→ PDF figures are kept: each one is saved as a PNG next to the markdown and referenced at its caption.
→ A `figures` list in the result (file, page, caption), with per-document limits and a report of anything skipped.
→ PDFs with images no longer quietly fall back to plain text extraction.

## Pinned-issue

**arcus-provider-runtime v0.8.0 released — PDF figures are kept**

What you'll notice as a consumer:

1. PDF extractions now come with a `<slug>.figures/` folder of PNGs, referenced in the markdown at each figure.
2. `extractor_detail["figures"]` lists them (file, page, caption, size); `figures_skipped` says what was left out and why (decorative, over a limit).
3. Limits are configurable (`FigureOptions`, or `--max-figures N` on the CLI; `0` keeps none).
4. HTML captures on a machine without Playwright's browser now fail with a message telling you to run `playwright install chromium`.

Full changelog: https://github.com/jivebug/arcus/blob/main/CHANGELOG.md#080--2026-11-22

## Substack / newsletter

Research PDFs are mostly figures and captions; until now arcus kept the captions and threw the pictures away. 0.8.0 keeps them — saved as images and placed back in the markdown where they appeared — so a paper's charts travel with its text.
